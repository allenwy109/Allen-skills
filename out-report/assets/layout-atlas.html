布局决策图谱

这是从已批准 A4 黄金小册抽取的线框与选型约束，不是最终报告模板或渲染器能力声明。线框只表达阅读顺序、比例与容量边界。

Full evidence

Golden p09, p13

Reader task
Inspect one primary exhibit and its evidence.
Reading path
Claim → exhibit → evidence → source.
Capacity
Soft 4 / hard 6 evidence groups.
Trade-off
Maximum legibility; one question only.
Avoid
Competing exhibits.
Split
After the exhibit above six groups.
Whitespace
Clear exhibit and source zones.

Chart sidebar

Golden p05

Reader task
Read a chart with interpretation beside it.
Reading path
Claim → chart → sidebar → evidence.
Capacity
Soft 4 / hard 6 categories.
Trade-off
Interpretation costs plot width.
Avoid
Long sidebar prose.
Split
Move detailed evidence onward.
Whitespace
10mm conceptual gutter.
